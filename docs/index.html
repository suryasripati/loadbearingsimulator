How it works and where it is weak

  • Adoption follows an S-curve. Layer revenue is the value pool times adoption times the layer's share, with the share drifting each year (negative drift stands for commoditisation).
  • Build capex is spread over the build years. After that, sustaining spend equals build capex divided by asset life. Split the life of the technology from the life of the asset when you set it.
  • Present value uses your discount rate. The break-even premium solves for the entry price at which present value is zero.
  • This is a straight-line model. There is no competition between layers, no price war feedback, and no check that shares add up beyond the warning above.
  • Demand evidence is your judgement on a 1 to 5 scale. The model does not verify it.
  • Not yet done: scoring past episodes (British railways, fibre, dot-com applications) blind, using only what was knowable at the time. Until then, treat outputs as a way to see which assumptions carry the result, not as calibrated odds.
