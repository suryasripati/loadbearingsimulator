Calibration Scaffold

Load Bearing Simulator

A structure for scoring past episodes using only what was knowable at the time: draft the inputs with cited sources, lock them, and only then record what happened. Back to the simulator.

Scaffold only, no data. No episode is filled in, and no number here may be invented: every numeric input needs a cited source. Episodes stay in this browser; nothing is fetched or sent. Not financial advice.

Episodes

Episode

Locking is not blocked. If you lock anyway, this acknowledgement and the counts are recorded in the lock and covered by its hash.

Episode and rules

Sources

Inputs may cite only sources published on or before the as-of date. A source compiled later from period data counts for inputs only if its series is truncated on or before the as-of date. Outcomes must cite sources published after it.

Settings

Each number needs a basis. Sourced and derived values need at least one cited source (comma-separated source ids), and derived values show their calculation. A judgement needs a rationale; its sources are optional, but an uncited judgement is labelled and keeps the episode file out of the repository.

Layers

Outcomes

Entered only after locking. For each layer: did capital earn its cost? Yes and no need a source; contested needs at least one citation on each side; every outcome needs a note. Outcome sources must be published after the as-of date.

Model verdict against recorded outcome

Verdicts are recomputed with the current model. Where an episode was locked under a different model version, the row says so.
